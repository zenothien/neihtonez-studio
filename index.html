Desktop Creator Companion

Review, verify, and publish vertical short-form videos to TikTok.

Neihtonez Studio is a desktop workflow tool built for creators to inspect rendered 9:16 animated stories, verify platform compliance and AI disclosure labels, and publish approved videos directly through the official TikTok Content Posting API.

1. Official TikTok Login Kit

Authenticates securely via OAuth 2.0 with PKCE so creators authorize posting only for their own account.

2. Human Review & Compliance

Displays video metadata, captions, hashtags, and privacy controls so creators approve every post before upload.

3. Direct Post & Inbox Drafts

Supports both Direct Post (video.publish) and TikTok Inbox Drafts (video.upload) without watermarks.

How It Works

  • Sign in with your TikTok account using Login Kit (user.info.basic).
  • Inspect your rendered MP4, clean caption, and creator posting options fetched from creator_info/query.
  • Select your desired privacy level, interaction permissions, and disclosure settings, then confirm upload.
