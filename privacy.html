Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026 · Application: Neihtonez Studio

1. Overview

Neihtonez Studio respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Application accesses when you authenticate with TikTok and how that information is handled.

2. Data Accessed via TikTok APIs

When you authorize Neihtonez Studio via TikTok Login Kit, the Application requests only the minimum scopes needed:

  • user.info.basic & Creator Info: Used to read your TikTok Open ID, display name, username, and account posting capabilities (such as available privacy options and interaction settings).
  • video.upload & video.publish: Used solely to upload video files and captions that you explicitly select and confirm for publishing or saving as a draft on your TikTok account.

3. Local Storage & No Third-Party Sharing

Neihtonez Studio operates locally on your desktop device. OAuth 2.0 access tokens, refresh tokens, and upload receipts are stored locally on your machine and transmitted exclusively to TikTok's official API endpoints (open.tiktokapis.com). We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or tokens with any third-party analytics, advertising, or external servers.

4. Data Retention & Revocation

You may revoke Neihtonez Studio's access at any time from your TikTok account settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions), and you may delete locally stored token files from your computer at any time.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or data handling, contact the operator at https://www.tiktok.com/@neihtonez.
